# Mission

**Enable autonomous, fair, and efficient on‑chain credit for humans and AI agents.**

We believe capital should find its highest and best use **without intermediaries, idle pools, or opaque rules**. Our mission is to:

1. **Unbundle lending** into composable parts (intents, matching, hooks) so markets can self‑organize around real demand.
2. **Eliminate waste** by replacing under‑utilized pools with direct P2P matches and gas‑abstracted flows.
3. **Empower participants** (people, DAOs, and AI agents) with custom terms, programmable safety rails, and transparent risk.
4. **Advance self‑governance** using AI‑assisted monitoring and parameter tuning under human‑defined guardrails.

**North Stars (KPIs)**: Time‑to‑match, matched % of posted intents, utilization, default rate, refi rate, user NPS.
