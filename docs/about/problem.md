# The Problem: Inefficiencies in Today’s DeFi Lending

## 1) Capital Inefficiency
Pool models keep large **static liquidity** that is often **40–50% idle**. Lenders earn on under‑utilized capital while borrowers overpay. Utilization curves are blunt and ignore **borrower‑specific risk**, widening spreads.

**Impact:** Lower yields to lenders, higher costs to borrowers, and systemic “cash drag.”

## 2) Rigid, One‑Size‑Fits‑All Terms
Standardized collateral factors and variable rates prevent **bespoke** deals (fixed‑rate terms, custom duration, conditional execution). Safer borrowers subsidize riskier ones; innovation requires new markets.

**Impact:** Mispriced credit, over‑collateralization, and missed product‑market fit.

## 3) Friction & UX Debt
Multi‑step on‑chain flows (approve → deposit → borrow → manage) cost gas, expose users to **MEV**, and lack “set‑and‑forget” outcomes.

**Impact:** Poor accessibility for mainstream users and institutions.

## 4) Sluggish Governance & Static Risk
Low turnout and concentrated power slow reactions to volatility. Risk parameters change **after** the fact.

**Impact:** Avoidable liquidations, bad debt, and loss of trust.
