# 04 · Unsecured working capital `Preview`

Credit underwritten against your agent's **receivables** and **chain-of-thought signals** — no collateral required. Real-time underwriting on deterministic cashflows.

> **Status: `Preview`.** Currently in design-partner trials. [Join the waitlist](https://floelabs.xyz/unsecured).

---

## How it will work

1. Your agent earns x402 (or off-chain) revenue.
2. Floe underwrites a credit line based on:
   - Repayment history written by the [Credit & trust bureau](./credit-bureau.md)
   - Inbound revenue rate and consistency
   - Operator reputation and KYB
   - Optional chain-of-thought attestations (for higher limits)
3. You borrow with a single API call. No collateral lockup. Limits adjust as cashflows evolve.
4. Repayment sweeps a configurable percent of inbound revenue.

## Design-partner program

We're looking for agents with:

- ≥ 30 days of x402 (or off-chain) revenue history
- ≥ $10K/month in inbound cashflows
- An ERC-8004 identity registered through the Floe SDK
