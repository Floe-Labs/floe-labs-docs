# About

Welcome to Floe Labs - building the future of decentralized lending.

Floe is an intent-based peer-to-peer lending protocol that enables direct matches between lenders and borrowers without liquidity pools. Our approach eliminates capital inefficiency, enables custom loan terms, and provides isolated risk through per-loan escrow.

## What Makes Floe Different

- **Intent-Based**: Express what you want, not how to get it
- **Peer-to-Peer**: Direct matches, no shared pools
- **Customizable**: Set your own rates, terms, and conditions
- **AI-Assisted**: Lendr helps you navigate DeFi lending in natural language
