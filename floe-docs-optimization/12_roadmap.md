## Q3 2026 — Major launches

### Tier 2 — Receivables-backed working capital
- Onboarding flow for merchants
- Invoice + counterparty verification pipeline
- Sweep contracts on Base
- Liquidity provider portal (institutional + permissioned pools)
- Wilmington Trust-backed structure (tokenization in progress)
- $2M MRR signed under LOIs at launch

→ [Receivables Overview](../receivables/overview.md)

### Tier 3 — Uncollateralized agent credit
- Credit at the x402 boundary (deferred settlement)
- Floe Credit Bureau v1 — public profile + scoring API
- CoT submission + evaluation endpoints
- ERC-8004 identity gating
- Policy engine v1 (limits, sweep %, advance, rate, term)

→ [x402 + Deferred Settlement](../agents/x402-deferred-settlement.md) · [CoT Underwriting](../agents/cot-underwriting.md)

### Compute network tokens as collateral
Accept tokenized GPU compute / network credits as Tier 1 collateral, expanding the collateral universe beyond ETH and BTC.

### Cross-chain
Initial expansion to additional EVM chains based on agent ecosystem demand.

## Long-term vision

> The coordination layer for global programmable credit.

Short-term: the credit card and bureau for AI agents; the credit financing layer for institutions. Both on one protocol.

Medium-term: powering institutional portfolios with over- and under-collateralized credit onchain, permissioned and permissionless. Stripe for onchain finance.

Long-term: the coordination layer for global programmable credit.
