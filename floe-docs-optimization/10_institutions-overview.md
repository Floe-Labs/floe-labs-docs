## What you're underwriting

Floe originates credit against deterministic onchain cashflows:

- **Tier 2 — Merchant receivables.** Lien on signed invoices and pipeline. Daily/weekly remittance. Wilmington Trust-backed structure. Target ~30% net IRR.
- **Tier 3 — Agent compute / working capital.** Per-call and short-term lines extended to AI agents with onchain revenue and verifiable identity. Higher frequency, smaller ticket, structured around bureau-driven underwriting.

Both share the same primitive — onchain liens on deterministic future cashflow with smart-contract-enforced repayment.

## Risk framework

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Borrower default (Tier 2) | Counterparty credit checks; advance % capped; concentration limits; bureau performance tracking |
| Borrower default (Tier 3) | CoT feasibility check; sweep mechanic from inbound revenue; bureau-gated limits |
| Smart contract risk | Ongoing audits; bug bounty; circuit breakers |
| Oracle risk | Dual oracle (Chainlink + Pyth); auto-pause on anomalies |
| Liquidity risk | Daily / weekly remittance profile; redemption windows defined per pool |
| Regulatory risk | Permissioned pools; KYB; ongoing legal review |
